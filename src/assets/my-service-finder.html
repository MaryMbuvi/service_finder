In case of crisis:

If you are in an urgent crisis, we have compiled a list of immediate, confidential helplines that do not require parental permission.

Safety & Abuse

LoveIsRespect (Teen Safety)

100% confidential space to text or talk if a partner is threatening, controlling, or hurting you.

Text 'LOVEIS' to 22522 / Call 1-866-331-9474
Mental Health

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Free, confidential, 24/7 support via call or text if you are feeling completely overwhelmed.

Call or Text 988
LGBTQ+ Support

The Trevor Project

24/7 suicide prevention and mental health support built purely for LGBTQ+ young people.

Text 'START' to 678-678 / Call 1-866-488-7386
Sexual Health

Planned Parenthood Direct

24/7 direct connection to talk with vetted counselors about birth control, tests, or missing periods.

Call 1-800-230-7526 (PLAN)
